Property tax

In Oregon, property tax is based on the home's assessed value, which does not reset when the home sells. Two homes with the same price can have very different tax bills. For an accurate number, switch the tax field to $ and enter the current tax bill from the listing or PortlandMaps.

Estimates use average effective property tax rates (Tax Foundation 2026, from 2024 Census ACS data), with county-level rates for Portland and Vancouver ZIPs. Your actual rate may differ a lot. Check your local assessor and override the rate above if you know it.
